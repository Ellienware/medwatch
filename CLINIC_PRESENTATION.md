## How It Works - Patient Journey

### Step 1: Patient Registration (Receptionist)
```
Your receptionist opens the app and in 2 minutes:
- Captures patient demographics
- Scans ID document
- Records employer and job role
- Obtains POPIA consent
- Takes patient photo
- Schedules appointment

Patient is in the system. Ready for their medical.
```

### Step 2: Check-In & Vitals (Receptionist)
```
Patient arrives:
- Receptionist marks as "Checked In"
- Captures vitals (blood pressure, weight, height)
- Assigns to nurse for clinical tests

Real-time status updates visible to everyone.
```

### Step 3: Clinical Tests (Nurse)
```
Nurse sees patient in their queue:
- Conducts audiometry test
- Performs spirometry
- Vision screening
- Drug & alcohol test
- Captures all results digitally
- Flags abnormal results
- Adds clinical notes
- Marks patient as "With Doctor"

Doctor immediately sees patient is ready.
```

### Step 4: Medical Examination (Doctor)
```
Doctor opens patient file:
- Reviews all test results (color-coded)
- Sees nurse notes
- Reviews medical history
- Conducts examination
- Makes determination: Fit / Unfit / Fit with Restrictions
- Issues digital certificate
- Adds recommendations

Certificate generated in 30 seconds.
```

### Step 5: Certificate Delivery (Automatic)
```
System automatically:
- Generates professional PDF certificate with:
  * Clinic branding
  * Test results summary
  * Doctor's digital signature
  * QR code for verification
- Emails certificate to employer
- Notifies employer in their portal
- Stores in patient's record
- Tracks expiry date

Employer has certificate within minutes. No phone calls.
```
